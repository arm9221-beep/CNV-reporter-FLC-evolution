CNV Reporter Evolution Under Fluconazole

By Alyssah Mack and Saaz Sakrikar

Flow cytometry (Cytek) tracking of yeast populations carrying an mCitrine copy number variant (CNV) reporter, evolved in four media backgrounds at several fluconazole (FLC) concentrations. Each passage, mCitrine fluorescence is compared to 1-copy and 2-copy control strains to follow the formation of CNVs over time.

About the experiment

Populations are evolved in 96-well plates in four media and passaged every few days. At each passage the cells are read on the Cytek, and the mCitrine signal (B2-A), normalized to cell size (FSC-A), is used as a readout of reporter copy number. Every column on the plate is one media + FLC condition with its own controls:

  • Row B = 1-copy control, row C = 2-copy control
  • Rows D–F = experimental populations (Rep 1 / 2 / 3), each followed as its own population across passages

Passage and reading procedure

Every passage followed the same process:

  1. Growth: cells grow in the 96-well plate for at least two days.
  2. YPD dilution (morning of the reading): cells are diluted in YPD, 12 μL of cells into 110 μL of YPD.
  3. Outgrowth: the cells grow in YPD for 4.5–6.5 hours.
  4. Water dilution: the cells + YPD are diluted in water, and the plate is read on the Cytek.

The volume of water used in the final dilution was adjusted over the first passages until an optimal combination was found that made the reading most efficient. This change is not significant to the experimental results. It only affects how quickly the plate is read and the quality of the data.

SD media and passage numbering

SD media was added to the experiment at passage 4 (day 10), after the SCGlu, YPGly, and YPD populations had already been passaged three times. In the analysis, SD is numbered by its own passage count, so SD passage 1 is the plate read at passage 4 and lines up with passage 1 of the other media on every plot. The SD populations will continue to be evolved after the other media finish, so all media types will end up with the same number of passages.

Why YPD has no copy-number controls

The YPD wells do not have their own 1-copy and 2-copy controls. This is mostly because there was no space left on the plate, but also because CNV formation in YPD at FLC 16 is already well understood. The YPD FLC 0 populations essentially serve as the 1-copy control for YPD, since no CNVs are expected to form in these wells. YPD is therefore not gated in the analysis; it is shown as its raw mCitrine level over time, in the histograms, and on the FSC-A vs B2-A plots.

About this analysis

  • Singlets: saturated events are removed, and doublets are excluded using each well's SSC-A/SSC-H ratio.
  • Over time: the median B2-A of each well is plotted per passage alongside that column's 1-copy and 2-copy controls.
  • Histograms: B2-A/FSC-A distributions for each passage and replicate, overlaid with the control distributions, show any shift from 1 copy toward 2 copies.
  • Gates: non-overlapping rectangular 1-copy and 2-copy gates are fitted to the controls of each column on the FSC-A vs B2-A plot and applied to the experimental wells.
  • SpectroFlo export: the instrument-side SSC_S_2copy % Parent values are also plotted over time for comparison.

The raw .fcs files and SpectroFlo exports are not included on this site.
